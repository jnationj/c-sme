            Why this instead of asking a chatbot?

            • Current platform data, not memory. A chatbot answers from training data or whatever web pages it can open. We buy data about public TikTok and Instagram content for your question from third-party providers.
            • Every claim has a receipt. The report lists each data call with its on-chain transaction, so you can check what it was built from.
            • Price first, cents per question. No subscription. You see the total, for example $0.04, before anything is paid.
            • You can do this by hand for free, and that's fine. Both apps let you search posts and hashtags, and TikTok's Creative Center shows trending hashtags and top ads. What takes hours is reading dozens of posts, counting which hooks repeat and comparing accounts. We do that counting in about a minute and show the sources.
            • It tells you what it doesn't know. Each report ends with what the data can't answer, and the agent can't spend past hard limits.

            This is not a general assistant. It does one job and shows its evidence. How the agent works

                Kept safe by design

                • The AI writes. A rules engine, not the AI, approves every payment.
                • Hard caps: per call, per report, per day.
                • Only approved providers and tokens.
                • Never pays twice. Stops if a payment is uncertain.
                • Settlement limitation. The app relies on the facilitator's response and does not independently verify the incoming payment against a Celo receipt and token transfer event.
                • Every payment is logged with its transaction link.

                Who it's for

                Online sellers

                See what posts and accounts get attention in your niche, from fashion and beauty to food and gadgets, before you plan your next drop.

                Creators

                Find which hooks, formats and hashtags are working for accounts like yours, without scrolling through hundreds of videos.

                Small businesses

                Check who is active in your market and how they post, so you spend on ads with a clearer picture.

                cPilot reads public posts and accounts only. It does not see anyone's sales or private data.

                How it works

                1. 1 · Ask

                  Type a real question about your market, or start from an example.

                2. 2 · Review

                  See which data it will buy, what each source adds, and the exact price.

                3. 3 · Pay

                  Review the quote and approve a paid run in your wallet. Check the settlement status shown in the receipt.

                4. 4 · Read

                  Get a cited report with the limits stated and a receipt for every purchase.

                cPilot, built for the Agents on Open Rails hackathon on Celo. Reports describe public content and say what the data cannot answer. Verify before you rely on them. Data comes from third-party providers (TikHub and Apify), not from TikTok or Instagram directly. We have not verified how those providers collect it. Incoming settlement is not independently verified on-chain. See the project documentation before relying on payment status.
